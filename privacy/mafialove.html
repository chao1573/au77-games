隐私政策

本政策说明 AU77 在提供《Mafia Love》游戏及邮件客服时如何处理信息。联系我们：chao1573@qq.com。

游戏核心剧情离线运行，不要求注册 AU77 账户。本地剧情存档和偏好设置不会由游戏自动上传给 AU77。Apple 购买与恢复、主动发送的客服邮件，以及本网页的托管服务属于单独说明的处理场景。

1. 保存在设备上的信息

游戏在本机保存章节与节点进度、剧情选择、虚构人物关系数值、短信选择、照片线索检查、画面与结局收藏，以及语言和声音偏好。首次启动会读取系统语言以选择支持的界面语言；之后记住您选择的语言。这些数据用于恢复游玩和设置，不会被游戏自动上传到 AU77 的服务器，也不用于广告画像。

本游戏不提供自建账户或云存档服务。设备系统备份、应用商店备份或您自行复制文件可能另行保存这些数据，由您和相关平台控制。开发验证版本可以在本地记录运行错误；明确启用开发测试工具时还会生成本地测试截图或诊断摘要。这些文件不自动发送给 AU77。

2. Apple 购买与恢复

如果您使用 iOS 版本的章节购买或恢复功能，Apple StoreKit 负责交易及验证。游戏会使用 Apple 提供的商品标识、本地化价格、交易标识和购买权限状态，以提供相应内容、恢复购买及处理撤销。购买权限的来源是 Apple 的验证记录，重新开始故事不会清除已购权限。

游戏不会获取您完整的银行卡信息、Apple 账户密码或付款凭证密码，也不会把这些信息传给 AU77。Apple 可能根据其服务条款向开发者提供销售、财务或商店报表；这不意味着本游戏将您的剧情存档上传给开发者。Apple 独立处理的数据请参阅 App Store 与隐私。未启用内购的开发版本不会执行真实收费。

3. 邮件客服

只有当您主动向客服发送邮件时，AU77 才会收到您的发送邮箱、邮件内容，以及您自愿提供的截图、日志或其他附件。我们仅为回复问题、排查故障、处理购买支持或响应您的权利请求而使用这些信息。游戏不会自动附加存档、诊断文件或发送邮件。

请只提供处理问题所必需的信息，不要发送密码、银行卡资料或完整付款凭证。客服邮箱使用 QQ 邮箱服务；邮件传输及存储还涉及您自己的邮箱服务商。相关服务可能在不同国家或地区处理邮件，您应同时阅读其适用的隐私说明。我们不会出售客服个人信息，或将其用于个性化广告。

4. 引擎、权限与跟踪

本版本使用 Unity 引擎，但未启用 Unity Analytics、广告、云诊断或引擎诊断上报服务，也未接入 AU77 的用户行为分析服务器。游戏不使用广告标识符进行跨应用跟踪，不请求定位、通讯录、相册、相机或麦克风权限。游戏中的照片、短信和人物均为内置虚构内容，不需要访问设备中的真实照片或消息。

操作系统、应用商店及您自行使用的备份或诊断功能可能依其各自设置处理信息。本政策不取代这些平台的隐私政策。若未来添加会改变信息处理方式的服务，我们将在启用前更新说明，并按适用法律取得必要授权。

5. 保存期限与安全

本地存档和设置保留到您清除相应数据或删除其所在存储位置为止。重新开始会重置剧情进度及收藏，但保留语言、声音偏好和有效购买权限。客服邮件及附件仅保留至解决问题、处理相关争议及履行适用法律义务所必要的期间；可通过客服请求删除，法律要求保留的资料除外。

我们采用与本服务相适应的访问控制和存储保护措施。没有任何存储或传输方式能保证绝对安全；请妥善保护设备和您自己的备份。

6. 您的选择与权利

您可在设置中更改语言和声音偏好，或重开故事。删除全部应用数据会移除本地存档及偏好，请先确认是否需要自行备份。Apple 已验证的购买记录由 Apple 管理，可通过其恢复功能重新获取有效权限。AU77 无法远程读取或删除未提供给我们的本地存档。

根据适用法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理或投诉等权利。关于 AU77 收到的客服信息，请向 chao1573@qq.com 提出请求；我们会在必要的身份核验后按适用规则响应。某些权利需要向 Apple、邮箱或网站托管服务商直接提出。

7. 未成年人

本游戏为剧情与恋爱题材，不作为专门面向儿童的服务。请参考您所在地区应用商店显示的年龄分级；未达到当地独立使用或同意年龄的用户应在监护人指导下使用。我们不明知地通过客服收集不具备相应同意能力的儿童资料。若您认为存在此类情况，请联系我们处理。

8. 本网页与政策更新

本政策网页本身不包含广告、用户分析脚本、跟踪像素或 Cookie。承载网页的托管服务商可能根据其服务配置处理访问日志，例如 IP 地址、浏览器信息和访问时间；这与游戏的离线存档相互独立。使用页面上的外部链接后，相应网站按其自身政策处理访问。

我们会在本页发布政策更新并修改上方日期。对于信息用途的实质变化，将根据适用要求提供额外通知或取得相应授权。疑问请联系 AU77：chao1573@qq.com。

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AU77 handles information when providing Mafia Love and email support. Contact: chao1573@qq.com.

The core story runs offline and does not require an AU77 account. The game does not automatically upload local story saves or preferences to AU77. Apple purchases, emails you choose to send, and the hosting of this policy page are separate situations described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves chapter and node progress, story decisions, fictional relationship values, message choices, photo-clue checks, artwork and ending collections, and language and audio preferences locally. On first launch it reads the system language to choose a supported interface language, then remembers your selection. These records restore gameplay and settings; the game does not automatically upload them to an AU77 server or use them for advertising profiles.

There is no AU77 account or cloud-save service. Operating-system or app-store backups, or files you copy yourself, may separately retain this data under your control and the platform's settings. Development builds can record local runtime errors; explicitly enabled development test tools also create local test screenshots or diagnostic summaries. These files are not automatically sent to AU77.

2. Apple purchases and restoration

If you use chapter purchases or restoration on iOS, Apple StoreKit handles the transaction and verification. The game uses product identifiers, localized prices, transaction identifiers and entitlement status supplied by Apple to provide content, restore purchases and handle revocations. Verified Apple records determine ownership; restarting the story does not remove valid purchases.

The game does not receive your full payment-card details, Apple Account password or payment-authentication secrets, and does not send them to AU77. Apple may independently provide developers with sales, financial or store reports; this does not mean that the game uploads your story save. For Apple's processing, see App Store & Privacy. Development versions with purchases disabled do not make real charges.

3. Email support

AU77 receives your sender address, message and any screenshots, logs or attachments you voluntarily supply only when you contact support. We use these to answer questions, investigate problems, provide purchase support or respond to rights requests. The game does not automatically attach saves or diagnostic files, or send emails.

Provide only information necessary to resolve your issue. Do not send passwords, card details or complete payment credentials. The support mailbox uses QQ Mail; sending and storing email also involves your own email provider. These services may process email in different countries or regions, and their applicable privacy information also matters. We do not sell support personal information or use it for personalized advertising.

4. Engine, permissions and tracking

This version uses Unity but does not enable Unity Analytics, advertisements, cloud diagnostics or engine-diagnostics reporting, and does not integrate an AU77 behavioral-analytics server. The game does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking or request access to location, contacts, photos, the camera or microphone. In-game photos, messages and characters are built-in fictional content, not your device's actual photos or messages.

Your operating system, app store, and backup or diagnostic functions you choose may independently process information under their own settings and policies. This policy does not replace those policies. If future services change our information handling, we will update the notice and obtain any authorization required by applicable law before enabling them.

5. Retention and security

Local saves and preferences remain until you clear the corresponding data or remove their storage location. Restarting resets story progress and collections while retaining language, audio preferences and valid entitlements. Support emails and attachments are retained only as necessary to address the issue, related disputes and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion, except where retention is legally required.

We use access controls and storage safeguards appropriate to this service. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Protect your device and any backups you create.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change language and sound settings or restart the story. Clearing all app data removes local saves and preferences, so consider whether you need your own backup first. Apple manages its verified purchase records, and its restoration functionality can restore valid entitlements. AU77 cannot remotely inspect or delete local saves that you have not supplied to us.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, or complain to a supervisory authority. For support information received by AU77, contact chao1573@qq.com. We will respond under applicable rules after any necessary identity checks. Some requests must be directed to Apple, your email provider or the website host.

7. Younger users

This story and romance game is not a service specifically directed at children. Follow the age rating displayed in your regional app store. Users below the local age for independent use or consent should use it with guardian guidance. We do not knowingly collect support information from children who cannot provide the relevant consent. Contact us if you believe this has occurred.

8. This page and policy changes

This policy page itself contains no advertisements, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies. Its hosting provider may process access logs, such as IP addresses, browser details and access times, according to its service configuration. This is separate from the game's offline saves. External links lead to websites whose own privacy policies apply.

Updates will be published here with a revised date. For material changes to information use, we will provide additional notice or obtain authorization where required. Questions: AU77, chao1573@qq.com.
