章节与购买

第 1 章免费，一次性应用内购买解锁第 2–6 章，无需订阅。价格由 App Store 显示。重新开始故事不会清除 Apple 验证的已购权限。

已经购买但章节仍锁定

确认设备使用购买时的 Apple 账户并连接网络，在游戏“设置”的 App Store 购买区域选择“恢复购买”，或使用章节解锁弹窗中的恢复入口。不要为了排查问题重复购买。恢复购买用于恢复章节权限，不会恢复另一台设备上的剧情存档。

暂时无法连接 App Store

检查网络，稍后重新打开购买页面或使用连接重试入口。若问题持续，请邮件提供具体提示及版本信息。核心剧情可离线阅读，购买与恢复购买需要 App Store 连接。

进度、语言与声音

游戏在本机保存剧情和偏好设置，可在游戏中存档与读取。首次启动根据系统语言选择受支持语言，此后记住你的选择。语言和声音可在设置中调整。游戏不提供自建账户或云存档；删除应用或清除应用数据可能导致本地进度丢失，排查前请谨慎操作。

退款与隐私

Apple 购买退款请前往 Apple 问题报告页面按提示申请，是否获批由 Apple 决定。个人信息处理方式请参阅 隐私政策。

Chapters and purchases

Chapter 1 is free. One in-app purchase unlocks Chapters 2–6, with no subscription. The App Store displays your local price. Restarting the story does not remove ownership verified by Apple.

Purchased chapters are still locked

Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Select Restore Purchases in the App Store purchases section of the game's settings or in the chapter unlock dialog. Avoid purchasing again to troubleshoot. Restoring purchases recovers chapter access, not story saves from another device.

The App Store is unavailable

Check your connection and reopen the purchase page later, or use its connection retry control. If the issue persists, email us the exact message and version details. The core story can be read offline; purchases and restoration require an App Store connection.

Progress, languages, and audio

The game stores progress and preferences locally and provides save and load controls. It initially selects a supported language based on your system language, then remembers your choice. Adjust language and audio in settings. There is no AU77 account or cloud save service. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete local progress; take care when troubleshooting.

Refunds and privacy

For Apple purchases, request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem service. Apple decides eligibility. See our Privacy Policy for information about data handling.
